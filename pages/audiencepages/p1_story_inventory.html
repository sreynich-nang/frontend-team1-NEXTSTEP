The Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC)—or (សាលាតិចណូ) as almost everyone calls it—is widely recognized as the premier engineering and technology university in Cambodia.

Founded in 1964 with support from the Soviet Union, it has evolved into a highly respected public institution that builds the country's technical workforce.
