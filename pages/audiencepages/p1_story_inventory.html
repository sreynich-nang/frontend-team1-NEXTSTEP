Why they match: basic configuration requirements like server systems or heavy power frameworks require professional collaboration. In an engineering university setting like ITC, the role of a teacher changes depending on the hour. You typically experience them in two distinct environments:

  • The Lecture Hall: As seen above, the teacher utilizes digital displays, project architectures, and slides to introduce complex logic, database structures, or programming concepts while students follow along on their laptops.
  • The Practical Lab: The teacher shifts from a lecturer to a hands-on mentor, moving between benches to help student teams debug their code, malfunctioning laboratory hardware.
