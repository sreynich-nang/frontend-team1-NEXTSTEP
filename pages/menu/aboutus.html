Our story

Why we built this website

The idea for NextStep came from our own experiences. At different stages of life, we often asked ourselves, "What should I do next?" and "Where can I find real advice?" We realized that useful information is often scattered and not tailored to students in Cambodia. That's why we created NextStep—a platform where people share their real-life journeys to help others make informed decisions with confidence.

Mission

Our Journey :

Our journey started as a university project with a vision beyond the classroom. We researched students' challenges and created NextStep, a platform that organizes real-life experiences into clear learning paths. Students can explore stories about high school, choosing a major, first jobs, and scholarship journeys. Our goal is to make NextStep Cambodia's trusted platform for student guidance and experience sharing.
